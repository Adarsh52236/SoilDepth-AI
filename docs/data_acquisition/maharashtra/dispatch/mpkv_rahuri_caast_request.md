### Subject: Formal Request for High-Resolution IoT Soil Moisture Telemetry Data for Academic Research on Root-Zone Soil Moisture Reconstruction (SoilDepth-AI)

Respected Sir,

We write to formally submit an academic data requisition for multi-depth in-situ soil moisture observations collected by the precision sensor networks at Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth (MPKV), Rahuri, particularly under the prestigious **ICAR-NAHEP CAAST-CSAWM** initiative (`MPKV-RAHURI-CAAST`).

#### 1. Context and Academic Motivation
The **SoilDepth-AI** project is a non-commercial academic research undertaking focused on estimating vertical root-zone soil moisture ($0\text{--}60\text{ cm}$) using multimodal satellite remote sensing, weather reanalysis, and machine learning. MPKV Rahuri represents the agronomic and hydrological heart of Western Maharashtra’s canal-irrigated and dryland Deccan Plateau, conducting world-class research on major regional crops including sugarcane, wheat, chickpea, and soybean.

The IoT-based automated soil moisture telemetry infrastructure deployed by CAAST-CSAWM (including the Precision Smart Soil Moisture Monitoring System - PSMISS and capacitive probe arrays) provides an ideal high-assurance ground-truth benchmark to validate AI-driven soil moisture reconstruction.
