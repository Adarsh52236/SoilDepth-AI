## 2. Nine-State Taxonomy Definition

Every station and institutional requisition in SoilDepth-AI is governed strictly by the following 9-state state machine:

```
[MERELY_IDENTIFIED]
        │
        ▼ (Drafting initiated)
[DRAFTED_NOT_SENT]
        │
        ▼ (Attachments verified & contacts audited)
[READY_FOR_SUBMISSION]  ◄── CURRENT STAGE FOR 5 PACKETS
        │
        ▼ (Documentary submission proof logged)
[REQUEST_SUBMITTED]
        │
        ▼ (Institutional response received)
[ACKNOWLEDGED]
        │
        ├──► (Approved / Invoiced / MOU signed) ──► [APPROVED]
        │                                                │
        │                                                ▼ (Data files delivered)
        │                                           [DATA_RECEIVED]
        │                                                │
        │                                                ▼ (Passed 10 ingestion gates)
        │                                           [DATA_VERIFIED]
        │
        └──► (Declined / Incompatible) ───────────► [DATA_REJECTED]
```

### Definitions:
1. **`MERELY_IDENTIFIED`:** Candidate station or observational network identified in peer-reviewed literature or registries; no requisition documents drafted.
2. **`DRAFTED_NOT_SENT`:** Requisition text drafted in markdown, but missing finalized administrative attachments, endorsements, or contact verification.
3. **`READY_FOR_SUBMISSION`:** Request letter finalized, verified against ground reality, attachments fully specified, contacts verified, awaiting PI physical signing and dispatch.
4. **`REQUEST_SUBMITTED`:** Formally transmitted via official portal, institutional email, or registered post, backed by archival submission evidence.
5. **`ACKNOWLEDGED`:** Target institution or government portal issues formal acknowledgment, ticket number, or proforma invoice.
6. **`APPROVED`:** Data requisition approved by competent authority; payment cleared or bilateral research agreement signed.
7. **`DATA_RECEIVED`:** Raw observational files and metadata physically received or downloaded into `dataset/Maharashtra/raw/`.
8. **`DATA_VERIFIED`:** Dataset successfully evaluated and passed all 10 validation gates of the SoilDepth-AI Ingestion Protocol.
9. **`DATA_REJECTED`:** Requisition formally rejected, data unrecoverable, or dataset permanently failed quality/depth compatibility gates.

## 3. Pre-Submission Quality Control Protocol

Before any packet transitions to `REQUEST_SUBMITTED`, the operator must complete this pre-flight verification:

- [ ] **Letterhead Printout:** The requisition letter must be printed on official university/institute letterhead bearing the institutional crest.
- [ ] **Investigator Credentials:** Research Supervisor and Lead ML Engineer signatures, full academic designations, and institutional email addresses must be physically affixed.
- [ ] **Annexure Bundling:** Annexures A through E must be stapled or compiled into a single high-resolution PDF document ($<10\text{ MB}$).
- [ ] **Zero Fabricated Claims:** Ensure no claims of prior institutional affiliation, existing access, or unauthorized sponsorship are present.
- [ ] **Evidence Archival Staging:** An evidence directory `dataset/Maharashtra/evidence/submissions/` must be ready to receive the timestamped delivery receipt or postal slip.

---

## 4. Post-Submission Archiving Protocol

Immediately upon dispatch:
1. Save the outbound email in standard RFC-822 `.eml` format containing full internet header traces.
2. Scan the signed university dispatch copy and the registered post / speed post receipt as a single PDF.
3. Record the transaction ID, speed post barcode, or DSP portal application number.
4. Store these evidence files under: `dataset/Maharashtra/evidence/submissions/<REQUEST_ID>/`.
5. Update the tracking status from `READY_FOR_SUBMISSION` to `REQUEST_SUBMITTED` in `ground_truth_request_tracker.md` and `station_registry.csv`.
