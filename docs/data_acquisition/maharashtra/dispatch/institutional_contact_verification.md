## 1. Governance & Verification Standards

To safeguard scientific rigor and adhere to institutional data governance standards, SoilDepth-AI enforces an absolute anti-fabrication mandate:
1. **Zero Contact Guessing:** No email address, telephone extension, or recipient name may be speculated, synthesized, or derived from informal sources.
2. **Documentary Evidence Mandatory:** Every contact channel must cite a publicly verifiable primary source (e.g., institutional `.res.in`, `.gov.in`, `.ac.in` directories, published peer-reviewed project papers, or Government of India official portals).
3. **Explicit Verification Taxonomy:** Every candidate channel is classified into:
   - `VERIFIED_INSTITUTIONAL_CONTACT`: Verified institutional office email / postal address from government/academic directories.
   - `VERIFIED_PORTAL_ROUTE`: Verified official government transaction portal with defined administrative workflow.
   - `VERIFIED_GOVT_CONTACT`: Verified state government project directorate contact.
   - `CONTACT_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`: Unverified or insufficiently evidenced contact routes (must NOT be used for dispatch until verified).
