### Subject: Official Academic Requisition for Soil Moisture and Agrometeorological Observatory Data for Scientific Research (SoilDepth-AI Project)

Respected Sir/Madam,

We write to formally requisition historical daily and hourly soil moisture observation records from the Central Agrometeorological Observatory operated by the India Meteorological Department at Pune (`IMD-AGRIMET-PUNE`), submitted via the official IMD Data Supply Portal (`https://dsp.imdpune.gov.in`).

#### 1. Research Project Context & Academic Objective
The **SoilDepth-AI** project is a non-commercial university research study developing high-assurance artificial intelligence models to map vertical soil moisture profiles ($0\text{--}60\text{ cm}$) using satellite microwave and optical observations. IMD’s Central Agrimet Observatory at Shivaji Nagar, Pune, represents one of India's longest-running, most rigorously calibrated standardized agrometeorological observation sites, providing essential reference measurements for national drought and hydrological assessments.

Access to verified in-situ soil moisture observations from this station is required to independently validate deep-layer predictive capabilities at standardized WMO/IMD depths.

#### 2. Station Details & Metadata
- **Station Identifier:** `IMD-AGRIMET-PUNE` (IMD Station ID / Index: `43063`)
- **Station Name:** Pune Central Agrometeorological Observatory (Agrimet)
- **Location:** Shivaji Nagar / College of Agriculture Campus, Pune, Maharashtra
- **Geographic Coordinates:** Latitude $18.5308^\circ\text{ N}$, Longitude $73.8507^\circ\text{ E}$
- **Benchmark Elevation:** $559.0\text{ m}$ AMSL

#### 3. Requisitioned Historical Observation Period
- **Start Date:** January 1, 2015 (`2015-01-01`)
- **End Date:** December 31, 2023 (`2023-12-31`)
- **Total Duration:** 9 continuous calendar years (3,287 days)

#### 4. Specific Parameters Requested
1. **Soil Moisture Observations:**
   - Volumetric soil water content ($\text{m}^3/\text{m}^3$ or % VWC) or gravimetric moisture percentage (% by dry weight) and bulk density at standard agromet depths:
     - $10\text{ cm}$
     - $30\text{ cm}$
     - $70\text{ cm}$
     - $100\text{ cm}$
     *(and $5\text{ cm}$, $20\text{ cm}$, $50\text{ cm}$ if instrumented on the experimental agromet plot)*.
   - Observation frequency: Daily morning (08:30 IST / 03:00 UTC) and evening (17:30 IST / 12:00 UTC) observations.
2. **Soil Temperature Profile:**
   - Daily soil temperature ($^\circ\text{C}$) at corresponding depths ($5, 10, 20, 30, 50, 100\text{ cm}$).
3. **Auxiliary Surface Agrometeorology:**
   - Daily 24-hour accumulated rainfall (mm).
   - Maximum and minimum dry bulb temperature ($^\circ\text{C}$).
   - Morning (08:30 IST) and evening (17:30 IST) relative humidity (%).
   - Daily pan evaporation (mm) from standard USWB Class A pan.
   - Sunshine duration (hours) from Campbell-Stokes recorder.
