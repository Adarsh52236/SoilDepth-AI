### Subject: Formal Request for In-Situ Multi-Depth Soil Moisture Profile Observations and Surface Flux Data for Academic Research on Root-Zone Soil Moisture Modeling (SoilDepth-AI)

Respected Sir,

We write to formally submit an academic data requisition for historical in-situ soil moisture observations and auxiliary micrometeorological flux data from the research observatories at the ICAR-NIASM campus, Malegaon, Baramati (`NIASM-BARAMATI-FLUX`).

#### 1. Context and Academic Motivation
The **SoilDepth-AI** project is an academic research effort dedicated to root-zone soil water estimation ($0\text{--}60\text{ cm}$) across drought-prone and water-stressed agricultural systems of Maharashtra. Baramati and the eastern Pune rain-shadow plateau represent a critical agro-ecological transition zone characterized by rocky basaltic subsoils, shallow Vertic Inceptisols, and intense abiotic moisture stress affecting sugarcane, rabi sorghum, chickpea, and orchard crops.

ICAR-NIASM’s state-of-the-art eddy covariance tower and multi-depth soil moisture sensor array offer an unparalleled ground benchmark to evaluate how satellite microwave remote sensing (Sentinel-1 SAR, SMAP L3) can capture root-zone water depletion under extreme atmospheric evaporative demand.

#### 4. Specific Parameters Requested
1. **Soil Moisture Profile (TDR / Capacitance Probes):**
   - Volumetric soil water content ($\text{m}^3/\text{m}^3$ or % VWC) at instrumented depth horizons:
     - Surface / Upper root zone: $10\text{ cm}$
     - Mid root zone: $20\text{ cm}$, $30\text{ cm}$
     - Deep root zone: $50\text{ cm}$ (and $100\text{ cm}$ if available)
   - Sampling frequency: Hourly continuous logs or daily averaged values.
   - Accompanying soil temperature profile ($^\circ\text{C}$) at corresponding depths.
2. **Surface Energy & Moisture Fluxes (Eddy Covariance Tower):**
   - Latent heat flux ($\lambda E$, $\text{W}/\text{m}^2$) and sensible heat flux ($H$, $\text{W}/\text{m}^2$).
   - Actual evapotranspiration ($ET_a$, $\text{mm}/\text{day}$).
   - Net radiation ($R_n$, $\text{W}/\text{m}^2$) and ground soil heat flux ($G$, $\text{W}/\text{m}^2$).
3. **On-Site Meteorological Baseline:**
   - Precipitation (mm), 2m air temperature ($^\circ\text{C}$), relative humidity (%), solar radiation ($\text{W}/\text{m}^2$), and wind speed ($\text{m}/\text{s}$).
4. **Soil & Management Metadata:**
   - Soil profile physical properties: bulk density ($\text{g}/\text{cm}^3$), sand/silt/clay fractions (%), field capacity, and permanent wilting point.
   - Sensor model, manufacturer (e.g. Campbell Scientific CS655 / Decagon 5TE), and calibration coefficients.
   - Field crop history and supplemental irrigation dates for the sensor plot.
