## 1. Overview of Data Governance in India

Unlike the United States (where datasets from USDA SCAN, USGS, NASA, and NOAA are completely open and unencumbered in the public domain), observational hydro-meteorological and in-situ ground-truth data in India operate under structured ministerial, institutional, and state-level data-sharing policies:

```
[Institutional Data Governance Landscape]
   ├── Category A: Open Online Portals (COSMOS-India, Copernicus, NASA Earthdata)
   ├── Category B: Paid Government Procurement via Bharatkosh (IMD National Data Centre)
   ├── Category C: State Project Governance (PoCRA PMU, Maharashtra Dept of Agriculture)
   └── Category D: ICAR & State Agricultural University MoUs (NBSS&LUP, NIASM, MPKV, VNMKV, PDKV, BSKKV)
```

## 2. Institutional Contact Directory & Application Protocols

### 2.1 India Meteorological Department (IMD) — National Data Centre (NDC)
* **Designated Office:** National Data Centre, Climate Research & Services (CRS), India Meteorological Department, Dr. Homi Bhabha Road / Ganeshkhind Road, Shivajinagar, Pune – 411005, Maharashtra.
* **Official Contact:**
  - *Email:* `data.service@imd.gov.in`
  - *Telephone:* +91 (20) 25572254 / +91 (20) 25572255
  - *Portal:* `https://dsp.imdpune.gov.in` (IMD Data Supply Portal)
* **Application Procedure:**
  1. Register an individual/institutional user account on the IMD Data Supply Portal.
  2. Upload valid institutional identification, a signed **Certificate of Undertaking**, and a formal requisition on institutional letterhead.
  3. Submit the specific parameters required: *Agro-AWS Soil Moisture (wfv) at 10 cm, 30 cm, 70 cm, 100 cm*, for selected stations (`IMD-AGRIMET-PUNE`, station code 43063), frequency (hourly/daily), period (2015–2023).
  4. Receive the official cost estimate generated by NDC.
  5. Process payment via the Government of India Non-Tax Receipt Portal (**Bharatkosh**, `https://bharatkosh.gov.in`):
     - *Ministry:* `081 - EARTH SCIENCES`
     - *Purpose:* `SUPPLY OF METEOROLOGY DATA WITH GST`
     - Add applicable $18\%$ Goods and Services Tax (GST).
  6. Upload the Bharatkosh transaction receipt PDF to the Data Supply Portal. Upon verification, data is released via direct portal download.

* **Designated Office:** Hydrometeorology Research Unit (HMRU) / Centre for Climate Change Research (CCCR), Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology, Dr. Homi Bhabha Road, Pashan, Pune – 411008, Maharashtra.
* **Official Contact:**
  - *Portal:* `https://cosmos-india.org`
  - *Email:* `hmru-cosmos@tropmet.res.in` / `cccr-data@tropmet.res.in`
* **Application Procedure:**
  1. Access `https://cosmos-india.org/data-portal/`.
  2. Complete free online researcher registration specifying academic/research affiliation.
  3. Download calibrated hourly volumetric water content ($0\text{--}30\text{ cm}$ CRNS integrated profile and Stevens Hydra Probe depths at 5 cm, 15 cm, 50 cm) for the Pune station.
  4. Sign standard open-data citation terms agreeing to acknowledge COSMOS-India, MoES, and UKCEH in published research.

### Template 1: Data Requisition to IMD National Data Centre, Pune

```text
[Official Institutional Letterhead]

Date: [DD/MM/YYYY]
Ref. No.: [PROJECT/DATA/IMD/2026/01]

To,
The Scientist-F & Head,
National Data Centre (NDC),
Climate Research & Services (CRS),
India Meteorological Department (IMD),
Ganeshkhind Road, Shivajinagar,
Pune – 411005, Maharashtra.
Email: data.service@imd.gov.in

Subject: Requisition for Historical Agro-AWS Soil Moisture & Surface Weather Data for Scientific Research on Climate-Resilient Agriculture in Maharashtra

Respected Sir/Madam,

We are conducting scientific research under Project 'SoilDepth-AI', focusing on the physics-informed AI modeling of multi-depth root-zone soil moisture to mitigate agricultural drought risks across Maharashtra's agro-climatic zones.

To calibrate and validate remote sensing and meteorological proxy models, we require historical observational records from the IMD Agro-AWS and surface meteorological network. Specifically, we request the following parameters for the period from 01-01-2015 to 31-12-2023:

1. Target Station:
   - IMD Central Agrometeorological Observatory, Pune (Station Index: 43063 / Shivajinagar)
   - Selected Agro-AWS stations across Maharashtra (Ahmednagar, Solapur, Parbhani, Akola, Nagpur)

2. Parameters Requested:
   - Soil Moisture (Water Fraction by Volume - wfv) at standard subsurface depths: 10 cm, 30 cm, 70 cm, and 100 cm (Hourly and Daily means).
   - Soil Temperature at standard subsurface depths: 10 cm, 30 cm, 70 cm, and 100 cm.
   - Synchronous surface meteorology: Rainfall (mm), 2m Air Temp (Max/Min), Relative Humidity (08:30 & 17:30 IST), Wind Speed, Solar Radiation.

This data will be used strictly for non-commercial academic research and algorithmic development. We enclose herewith our signed Certificate of Undertaking, institutional accreditation, and registration details on the IMD Data Supply Portal (Request ID: [DSP-XXXXX]).

We request you to kindly issue the proforma invoice / payment advice to enable remittance through the Bharatkosh Non-Tax Receipt Portal under Ministry 081 (Earth Sciences).

Thanking you.

Yours sincerely,

[Name & Signature]
Principal Investigator / Project Lead, SoilDepth-AI
[Institutional Designation & Seal]
```

```text
Greetings from the SoilDepth-AI research initiative.

SoilDepth-AI is developing high-resolution, multi-depth soil moisture reconstruction algorithms designed to estimate daily water availability across surface and root-zone strata (0–10 cm, 10–30 cm, and 50–100 cm) using multimodal Earth Observation satellites (Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 Optical, NASA SMAP) and meteorological reanalysis.

While initial model foundations have been verified, operational deployment across Maharashtra requires calibration against real-world in-situ ground measurements in the state’s predominant soil orders—specifically the heavy shrink-swell Vertisols (black cotton soils) of Vidarbha and Marathwada, alluvial soils of Khandesh, and lateritic soils of Konkan.

In this regard, we earnestly seek research collaboration with your esteemed university. We request access to historical, calibrated soil moisture observation records from your central research farms and AICRPDA/AICRPAM units for the period 2015–2023:
1. Soil Moisture Profile Records: Discrete depth intervals (0–15 cm, 15–30 cm, 30–60 cm, and deep subsoil layers) recorded under major dryland crops (Soybean, Cotton, Pigeon pea, Rabi Sorghum, Chickpea).
2. Associated Soil Characterization: Soil texture fractions (clay/silt/sand %), bulk density, and available water capacity (AWC).
3. Concurrent Weather Data: Daily rainfall, temperature, and pan evaporation from university observatories.

Terms of Collaboration & Academic Integrity:
- The data will be used exclusively for non-commercial model calibration and validation.
- Full academic attribution, co-authorship in peer-reviewed scientific publications, and acknowledgment of the University’s scientific contributions will be strictly ensured.
- All trained regional model weights and localized root-zone predictions will be shared back with the University to support your agro-advisory bulletins and farmer services.

We request an initial technical discussion to formalize a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between our institutions.
```
