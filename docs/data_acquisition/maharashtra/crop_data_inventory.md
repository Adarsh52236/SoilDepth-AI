## 1. Executive Summary: The 10 Target Crops of Maharashtra

## 2. Agronomic Profiles, Calendars & Phenological Root Dynamics

### 2.1 Soybean (*Glycine max*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** Latur, Nanded, Hingoli, Parbhani (Marathwada); Amravati, Yavatmal, Akola, Buldhana, Nagpur (Vidarbha); Kolhapur, Sangli (Western Maharashtra).
* **Calendar:**
  - *Sowing:* June 15 to July 15 (triggered by $\ge 75\text{--}100\text{ mm}$ cumulative monsoon rains).
  - *Emergence & Vegetative:* July to mid-August.
  - *Flowering & Pod Formation (Critical Water Stress Window):* August 15 to September 15.
  - *Harvesting:* September 25 to October 25.
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Rapid initial extraction in the top $0\text{--}20\text{ cm}$; root expansion reaches $40\text{--}60\text{ cm}$ at pod-filling. Dry spells during pod development severely impact yields if root-zone moisture below $20\text{ cm}$ falls below wilting point ($15\%$).

### 2.2 Cotton (*Gossypium spp.*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** The "White Gold" belt: Yavatmal, Wardha, Amravati, Akola (Vidarbha); Jalna, Aurangabad, Nanded, Parbhani (Marathwada); Jalgaon, Dhule (Khandesh).
* **Calendar:**
  - *Sowing:* June 15 to July 10 (dry sowing practiced in early June in Khandesh).
  - *Square Formation & Flowering:* August to September.
  - *Boll Development & First Picking:* October to November.
  - *Subsequent Pickings & Termination:* December to February.
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Taproot penetrates deeply into deep black cotton Vertisols, extracting moisture down to $80\text{--}120\text{ cm}$ during post-monsoon dry months (October–January). Model 1’s $50\text{ cm}$ depth and Model 2’s deep layers ($70\text{--}100\text{ cm}$) are essential for tracking boll maturation.

### 2.3 Rice / Paddy (*Oryza sativa*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** Konkan division (Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg, Raigad, Thane, Palghar); Eastern Vidarbha tank-irrigated belt (Bhandara, Gondia, Gadchiroli, Chandrapur); Western Ghats foothill strips (Kolhapur, Satara, Pune western talukas).
* **Calendar:**
  - *Nursery Raising:* Early June.
  - *Transplanting:* Late June to July (under standing water).
  - *Tillering & Panicle Initiation:* August.
  - *Grain Filling & Harvest:* October to November.
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Shallow fibrous root system concentrated in the top $0\text{--}25\text{ cm}$. Under puddle condition, saturation ($>0.40\text{--}0.50\text{ m}^3/\text{m}^3$) dominates.

### 2.4 Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** Irrigated canal and well tracts: Ahmednagar, Pune, Solapur, Satara (Western Maharashtra); Jalgaon, Dhule (Khandesh); Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Parbhani (Marathwada).
* **Calendar:**
  - *Sowing:* November 1 to November 25 (requires cool temperatures, night temp $<18^\circ\text{C}$).
  - *Crown Root Initiation (CRI - Most Critical Irrigation):* Day 20–25 after sowing.
  - *Tillering & Flowering:* December to January.
  - *Harvesting:* February 20 to March 20.
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Fibrous roots penetrate $60\text{--}90\text{ cm}$ in well-drained medium-to-deep black soils.

### 2.5 Sugarcane (*Saccharum officinarum*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** The Sugar Belt: Kolhapur, Sangli, Satara, Pune, Solapur, Ahmednagar (Western Maharashtra); Jalgaon (Khandesh); Nanded, Latur (Marathwada).
* **Three Sowing Regimes:**
  1. *Suru (Spring Planting):* Planted Jan–Feb; harvested Jan–March (12 months).
  2. *Pre-seasonal:* Planted Oct–Nov; harvested Dec–Feb (14–15 months).
  3. *Adsali (High-Yield Monsoon Planting):* Planted July–August; harvested Oct–Dec of following year (16–18 months).
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Extremely high water consumption ($2,000\text{--}2,500\text{ mm/year}$). Extensive fibrous root system active across the entire $0\text{--}100\text{ cm}$ profile. Continuously sustained by furrow or subsurface drip irrigation.

### 2.6 Maize (*Zea mays*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** Jalgaon, Dhule, Nandurbar (Khandesh); Ahmednagar, Pune (Western Maharashtra); Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Jalna (Marathwada).
* **Calendar:** Dual season: Kharif (Sown June–July, harvested Sept–Oct) and Rabi/Winter (Sown Oct–Nov, harvested Feb–March).
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Active root depth $60\text{--}80\text{ cm}$. Highly sensitive to both waterlogging during emergence and moisture stress during tasseling and silking.

### 2.7 Chickpea / Gram (*Cicer arietinum*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** Latur, Osmanabad/Dharashiv, Parbhani, Beed (Marathwada); Solapur, Ahmednagar (Western Maharashtra); Amravati, Akola, Buldhana (Vidarbha).
* **Calendar:**
  - *Sowing:* October 10 to November 10 (sown on receding monsoon residual soil moisture in deep Vertisols).
  - *Pod Development:* December to January.
  - *Harvesting:* February to March.
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Deep taproot system ($50\text{--}80\text{ cm}$) engineered by nature to extract residual subsoil moisture stored below $30\text{ cm}$ when surface soil ($0\text{--}10\text{ cm}$) has completely dried out. Tracking $20\text{ cm}$ and $50\text{ cm}$ moisture is critical for chickpea survival.

### 2.8 Pigeon pea / Tur (*Cajanus cajan*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** Parbhani, Latur, Jalna, Nanded (Marathwada); Akola, Amravati, Yavatmal, Wardha, Nagpur (Vidarbha).
* **Calendar:**
  - *Sowing:* June 15 to July 15 (almost universally intercropped in a 1:2 or 1:4 ratio with Soybean or Cotton).
  - *Slow Vegetative Growth:* July to September (shaded by companion crop).
  - *Companion Harvest & Rapid Branching:* October (companion crop harvested; pigeon pea branches out).
  - *Flowering & Pod Filling:* November to December.
  - *Harvesting:* Late December to February (150–180 days total duration).
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Deepest taproot among annual crops ($90\text{--}150\text{ cm}$). Extracts deep subsoil reserves stored during July–August monsoon down into deep basaltic saprolite.

### 2.9 Groundnut (*Arachis hypogaea*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:** Kolhapur, Sangli, Satara (Western Maharashtra); Dhule, Jalgaon (Khandesh); Raigad, Sindhudurg (Konkan Summer Groundnut).
* **Calendar:** Kharif (Sown June–July, harvested Oct) and Summer (Sown Jan–Feb, harvested May).
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Concentrated in the upper $30\text{--}50\text{ cm}$. Pegging and pod development require friable, moist surface soil ($0\text{--}10\text{ cm}$).

### 2.10 Sorghum / Jowar (*Sorghum bicolor*)
* **Agro-Climatic Hubs:**
  - *Kharif Sorghum:* Nanded, Parbhani, Yavatmal, Akola.
  - *Rabi Sorghum (Maldandi 35-1 - Renowned drought staple):* Solapur, Ahmednagar, Pune, Beed, Osmanabad/Dharashiv.
* **Calendar:**
  - *Kharif Jowar:* Sown June–July, harvested Oct.
  - *Rabi Jowar:* Sown September 15 to October 15 (on residual Vertisol profile storage), harvested January–February.
* **Root-Zone Extraction Profile:** Dense root network extending to $80\text{--}120\text{ cm}$. The classic model for soil moisture memory: Rabi jowar yield in Solapur is almost entirely determined by the depth of stored soil moisture at sowing time in late September.

## 4. Methodological Strategy: Integrating Crop Dynamics into SoilDepth-AI

Rather than treating soil moisture purely as a physical bare-soil column, crop canopy and phenology strongly modulate root-zone water extraction:

1. **Dynamic Canopy Proxy via Sentinel-2:**
   - Incorporating Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), Enhanced Vegetation Index (EVI), and Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI) tracks canopy leaf area expansion, transpiration surface, and crop senescence.
2. **Crop Mask Stratification:**
   - Utilizing ESA WorldCover 10m cropland masks ensures non-cropland areas (urban, barren basalt outcrops, water bodies) are masked out before spatial prediction.
3. **Multi-Depth Root-Zone Linkage:**
   - Deep taproot crops (Cotton, Pigeon pea, Rabi Sorghum) actively draw moisture from $50\text{--}100\text{ cm}$ while surface soil ($5\text{--}10\text{ cm}$) is desiccated. SoilDepth-AI’s discrete multi-depth formulation is tailored to distinguish this physiological decoupling.
